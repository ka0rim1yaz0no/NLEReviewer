# Situational choice and reference review

This release keeps 5,117 active situational questions, 20,468 choice explanations, 933 case groups, and 156 topics. It retains all question IDs and grading positions.

- The full-bank pass compared answer keys and every choice's reference provenance and searched for supporting passages. The case-level pass read each scenario group's primary decision, rather than reviewing only previously flagged questions. Before the edits, 359 of 382 cited URLs had extractable publisher or NLM text in the review cache; passage search found a candidate for 19,979 choice explanations. Other sources were checked through their publisher or a relevant official reference where available. A candidate passage does not itself verify a clinical claim.
- The reference-checked editorial changes cover 47 questions in 16 groups. They distinguish the stages of anaphylaxis, safe swallowing, IV insulin concentration and flow, hyperkalemia during infusion, magnesium toxicity, pediatric hypoglycemia, IMCI fast breathing, clozapine monitoring, leprosy neuritis, an obstructed chest drain, DKA, autonomic dysreflexia, and ventilatory support after opioid exposure. See `choice-review-data.ts` for the full rationales and primary citations.
- The fall-rate item now links to the relevant AHRQ toolkit. Quiz review labels each of the four explanations and links to named sources. The 156 guides keep each keyed decision but summarize 66 long reasoning notes and avoid repeating the same safe action in the safety note. No guide decision exceeds 200 characters; the quiz preserves the full clinical rationale.
- The prior three releases remain available for saved quizzes. New question wording only affects new attempts.

The source comparison and case review cover the entire bank. Individual clinical verification of every distractor explanation has not been completed.
